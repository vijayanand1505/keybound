## v0.5 MechKeys recordings

Source package: [MechKeys: Mechanical Keyboard Sounds](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/mechkeys-mechanical-keybo/ggkamagcddlombobgpfplaigbonfnpmi), version **2.0.2**. Recording credits in that package identify all shipped audio as CC0-1.0, sourced via [Clatterbox](https://github.com/zordhalo/clatterbox). Only recording files and their credits are included, with independent playback code.

- **Tactile:** StavSounds (12 presses), Foxfire- (release), alpinemesh (Enter), yottasounds (Backspace), sourced from Freesound. The exact source page for every file and the original license statement are preserved in [Tactile credits](public/audio/mechkeys/tactile/CREDITS.md).
- **Classic:** unicaegames, [Keyboard Soundpack 1](https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes), CC0-1.0. Sixteen individual keypress WAVs; [Classic credits](public/audio/mechkeys/classic/CREDITS.md).
- **Integrity:** [manifest](public/audio/mechkeys/manifest.json) records all 31 filenames, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Audio copies were verified byte-for-byte against the downloaded extension package; no re-encoding or resampling was applied to the files.

The per-pack credits are retained verbatim from the package. References there to research folders, calibration configuration or Clatterbox pack classes describe the upstream project; those folders/configuration are not included or used by KEYBOUND. The playback settings here follow MechKeys 2.0.2's behavior. MechKeys is identified only as the source of the requested profiles; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

## Game covers and presentation (v0.13)

Three new cinematic cover images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and copied into `public/images/games/`. They are served through Next.js Image optimization with responsive sizes and blur placeholders. They appear on the home page, game cards and the Signal Siege launch screen. The reactive SVG/Canvas artwork used during gameplay remains code drawn. These covers do not use images from ZType, other games or stock libraries.
